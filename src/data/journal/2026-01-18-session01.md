## Session Summary
- On a House Orien Lightning Rail train from Wroat to Sharn, the party first meets each other - for the second time
- Suspicious attackers in silver cloaks appear to sabotage the train with arcane bombs
- The party mitigates some of the damage from one bomb, and prevents the planting of the other two
- Two mysterious characters additionally attack the party, revealing themselves as changeling shapeshifters on death
